Our Philosophy

Why simple matters

Modern snacking became complicated with stabilizers, corn syrup, and palm oils. We believe nourishment belongs to simple sun, soil, and slow heat.

01
Sourced with care

Partnering directly with regional organic farms in Kashmir, Ladakh, and Mithila for seasonal, soil-first harvests.

02
Nothing added

Zero refined sugars, synthetic preservatives, artificial flavoring, or sulfur dioxide. Raw ingredients honor your body.

03
Packed small-batch

Milled and wood-roasted weekly in climate-sealed kraft paper pouches to preserve living enzymes and crunch.
